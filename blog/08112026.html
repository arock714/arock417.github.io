Hello reader,

I've recently realized that I'm not good at writing. I've had to write some cover letters for some job applications and it was taking me a good while of staring at a blank page or getting distracted before I really felt like I was making progress. Then I came up with a few sentences, reread them, realized they were absolute garbage, and deleted them. So I'm trying to get some practice in. I thought of making a substack and posting regularly on there but I didn't want complete strangers reading the garbage I came up with. So I'm writing here on my engineering portolio domain because its the only domain I own and I don't want to pay for another. Here, in plain html script, I am in the wild west of writing. Instead of Google Docs catching my spelling errors, gramatical errors, or complete incoherency, I am free write like crap. It's a freedom akin to throwing your phone out the window and leaping out the door to explore this crazy world and appreicate it's beauty without a safety net.

This is not a journal entry by the way. Even though it's beginning to flow like one. I will not be writing about my personal life. This is merely an attempt to practice the art of thinking through words. To assemble a main idea and support it. It's an extended writing excersize. (I'm pretty sure I spelled excersize wrong.) Back when I was in school I dreaded writing essays. I found it difficult. Sitting down to try to write was an exhausting process from which I would often get distracted. The notice of the hours passing without any fruitfull progress would frustrate me. I dreaded sitting down to write. But now that I'm out of school I realize what a privledge it was to have someone read your work. There are many aspiring writers who would die over and over again to have an observant and dedicated reader such as my english teachers. There I was, a kid who had been writing for half my life (5 years,) writing a piece of crap essay I didn't care about and I was so fortunate as to have it read and was given feedback by a caring and talented writing teacher! It's a real privledge that I didn't realize until I didn't have any more time left to appreciate it.

So that brings me to now. I'm realizing that writing is an important skill to have even in a world where a compter can do your writing and your thinking for you. It's an excersize of the mind that helps you think, formulate ideas, and brings into focus a much more profound idea than what you may have gone in with. It's an important skill for a human to have. I am a proud human and feel an imense responsibility to hone my writing skills through intentional practice. That's what this page is for.

Until the next one,

eric m
